# Aura – Reverse Engineering Task

Recreation of the "Aura – The Guardian of Accessibility" landing page, made as task 4 ("Reverse Engineer a Result") in my APL.

## What it is

A one-page concept website for a fictional accessibility tool for Windows. It is a demo – it does not change anything outside the browser.

## Features

- **Accessibility profiles** – dropdown with *The Voyager* (colour blind palette) and *The Beacon* (low vision: high contrast and bigger text).
- **Simulate Aura Enforcer** – marks all page elements with outlines and labels (UI Element, Structure, Interaction, Content box) and counts "fixes".
- **Clarify Content** – switches the text to simpler words and a more readable font.
